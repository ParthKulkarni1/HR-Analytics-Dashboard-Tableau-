# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard (Tableau)

## 🔍 Overview

This project showcases an **interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Tableau** to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and department-level trends.

The dashboard is designed to help HR teams and decision-makers understand **why attrition happens, which employee groups are most affected, and where retention efforts should be focused**.

It transforms raw HR data into clear, actionable insights through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📁 Dataset

* **Type:** HR Employee Dataset
* **Format:** Excel / CSV
* **Domain:** Human Resources Analytics

### Dataset includes:

* Employee demographics
* Department and job role
* Education background
* Gender and age
* Attrition status
* Job satisfaction ratings

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1️⃣ Employee Overview

Provides a summary of:

* Total employee count
* Attrition count
* Attrition rate
* Active employees
* Average age

### 2️⃣ Department-wise Attrition

Shows attrition across departments such as:

* HR
* R&D
* Sales

This helps identify which departments face the highest employee turnover.

### 3️⃣ Employee Distribution by Age Group

Displays employee count across different age groups to better understand workforce demographics.

### 4️⃣ Job Satisfaction Analysis

Presents job satisfaction ratings across various job roles, helping identify roles that may require retention focus.

### 5️⃣ Education Field-wise Attrition

Analyzes attrition based on employee educational background, including:

* Life Sciences
* Medical
* Marketing
* Technical Degree
* Human Resources

### 6️⃣ Attrition by Age Group and Gender

Shows attrition patterns across age groups segmented by gender to uncover high-risk employee segments.

### 7️⃣ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

* **Department**
* **Education**

These filters allow users to drill down into specific employee segments.
